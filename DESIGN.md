# My Lodge Design System

## Theme

Restrained, light hospitality system. Warm Ivory is the architectural surface; photography supplies the color and destination atmosphere.

## Color

- Canvas: `#F7F5F0`
- Ink: `#171717`
- Muted text: `#8A8A8A`
- Taupe: `#B7ADA0`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`

## Typography

Use Canela for English display moments, Neue Haas Grotesk (with Avenir Next fallback) for headings and Avenir Next for body copy. Korean glyphs fall back to Noto Sans KR, preserving Korean-first wrapping and readability. Use high contrast in size and weight, short metadata labels, and generous line-height.

## Components

Hairline rules, quiet text links, compact rectangular buttons, low-radius image frames, and white informational surfaces. Shadows are soft and rare.

## Motion

Slow opacity and transform reveals, contained image scale on hover, and existing reduced-motion behavior. No looping decorative motion, bouncy easing, or motion in form controls.

## Destination accents

Jumunjin uses cool ocean overlays. Yangyang uses low-saturation cedar and surf tones. Chiang Mai uses dark botanical shadows and sun-warmed photography, with a live/work editorial composition.
