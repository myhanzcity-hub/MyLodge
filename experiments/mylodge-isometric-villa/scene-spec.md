# Villa assembly concept and parts plan

**Size:** Hero-scale standalone SVG study; a single architectural model with a twelve-second assembly sequence.

**Concept:** A contemporary two-story seaside villa assembled from an architectural base, structural supports, facade panels, floor-to-ceiling glazing, balcony, layered roof, and an infinity-edge pool.

## Independently animated component groups

- Ground platform, perimeter line, villa foundation.
- Pool basin.
- Ground-floor slab, four columns, front wall, side wall, individual glazed panels, entry panel.
- Second-floor slab, four upper supports, front and side walls, separate glazing panels.
- Balcony slab, eight glass balustrade panels, drawn handrail.
- Lower roof, crown roof, individually drawn roof edges and ribs.
- Pool coping and tile course, clipped water plane, separate ripple paths.
- Soft contact shadows and individual interior-glow panes.

## Sequence

0–2 s foundation; 2–4 s ground floor; 4–6 s second floor and balcony; 6–8 s roof; 8–10 s pool finish and water fill; 10–12 s lighting and shadow settle. Construction uses translation, scaling, stroke-path drawing, and water-plane growth. Opacity is used only for light and shadows.
