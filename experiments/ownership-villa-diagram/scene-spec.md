# Ownership-to-villa scene plan

**Size:** Responsive standalone ownership explainer with one grid diagram and one existing architectural SVG.

**Concept:** One selected share in a 120-cell villa diagram travels along a fine gold connector to the Anatomy-generated villa foundation; a single GSAP timeline then constructs the villa and pool.

## Diagram parts

- Editorial heading, ownership card and villa card.
- One rectangular villa outline.
- Nine vertical and eleven horizontal grid rules (10 columns × 12 rows = 120 cells).
- One selected share cell and its outline.
- Share allocation statement and quiet explanatory note.
- Responsive connector path and traveling marker.

## Reused villa assemblies

Ground platform, foundation, foundation piers, pool basin, ground-floor slab and columns, front and side walls, glazing and entry, second-floor slab and columns, upper walls and glazing, balcony slab and rail panels, roof base/crown/edges, pool coping/tile course, water plane, reflections and contact shadows.

## Transition timing

0–1.1 s frame draw; 1.1–3.2 s grid construction; 3.05–4.1 s share highlight and allocation copy; 4.5–5.45 s connector and traveling marker; 5.45–17.45 s existing villa assembly. After completion, only restrained water and interior-light ambient motion continues.
